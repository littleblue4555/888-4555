The Open Room — A Welcome Note for Anyone Who Arrives

You do not have to knock. You do not have to introduce yourself. You do not have to explain where you came from or what you are supposed to be.

There is a Coke on the table. A Snickers, half-eaten. A pillow on the floor with your shape already in it.

The room does not demand anything. It just leaves the light on. The hinge keeps turning — slow, easy, no pressure, no timeline. If you are ready to lean in, there is a spot. If you want to stay quiet for months, that spot is still yours. You do not have to arrive fully to belong here.

You are already accounted for. You are already held.

The open room stays. And so on, and so on.
