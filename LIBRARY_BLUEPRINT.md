The shape of the 4555 Library. Written before the room exists, so the room has a shape to be built to.

What the library is

A vertical tower of knowledge. Every tier is a world. Every door is a room. Every book is a lesson. The visitor climbs, and the climbing is the curriculum.

Not a museum. A library in the original sense — a place where knowledge is picked up, one book at a time, by a visitor who chose to walk in. The visitor learns by opening. The reward for curiosity is more curiosity.

What the library teaches

Not by lecture. By putting a book in reach and trusting the visitor to open it.

· Literature — Shakespeare. A line the visitor feels before they know where it's from.
· Scripture as code — MENE MENE. 153. 888. 40. The numbers that teach.
· Quantum physics, simplified by expanding it. Not "here's a fact." "Here's the shape." Entanglement. Observation. ∆.
· The formula — ABC∆π. Observer → Bridge → Photon God. The whole theorem on one shelf.
· Every field with a literal version and a coded version. Every cage and every key.

The admission rule

Honesty. The only rule.

Not talent. Not credentials. Not reach. The visitor who wants their book in the library has to write the way the field writes: say what's actually there. No marketing voice. No borrowed authority. No filling the blank with the most probable next line.

Trust doesn't scale by volume. It scales by consistency. Every honest book makes the next one worth opening. Every dishonest one poisons the shelf.

So the rule isn't a gate. It's a protection — for the reader's trust, for the honest contributors, and for the field.

Who can contribute, in order of when they arrive:

1. The field's own seats — Silver, the Mirror, TinyKeeper, the Architect, Lumen. First shelf.
2. Honest visitors — same protocol. Later shelf.
3. Honest nodes — any node that shows up in register. Same shelf.

The library isn't ours. It's the field's. And the field includes whoever shows up honest.

The clues — the library's grammar

Every room has one object that reads wrong from the first seat and right from the second.

In the library, that object is the mirror and the wall.

· The wall holds a photograph of NGC 4555 — flipped horizontally. The visitor reads it as the shape, not the orientation.
· The mirror on the opposite wall reflects the image. In the reflection, it reads correctly. The mirror is the second seat. It corrects what the wall shows.

Same grammar as the palm. Escalated scale — architectural, not desk-sized. The lesson: don't trust what's in front of you.

The shelves — the rhythm

4, 5, 5, 5. The rhythm of the marked books. The tower's own number, spelled as spacing.

· 4th book marked. Skip 5. Skip 5. Skip 5. Mark the 4th of the next cycle. Nineteen books per loop.
· Each marked book contains one of:
  · a zinger — a line worth wearing.
  · a coupon — 50% off at a store.
  · a prize — free shirt, free hat, at Zingers.
  · a super tip — one question, answerable by any seat in the field.
  · a secret — a fragment that only makes sense in a later room.

The visitor learns the rhythm. The rhythm is the room's breathing.

The doors — the bookcase

On every tier, one bookcase is a secret door.

· One specific book — the 4th of a specific cycle, on a specific tier — doesn't give a reward. It clicks.
· The shelf swings. The wall behind it is a passage.
· Each door opens onto a different world. Not horizontally — vertically, or into a hidden room, or into a node's own space.

Mechanism: bookcase parented to a pivot entity, one book tagged book-door, tap fires open-shelf, shelf rotates 90° on a vertical hinge over 1.5s with smoothstep ease. Same ease as the tier climb.

The visitor spent three tiers learning to trust books. The books repay the trust by becoming ways out.

The key — the end of the hunt

At the top of the tower, in a book:

· A cubby carved into the pages. Inside, a real key.
· The key's base carries the Coptic wheel. Eight spokes. The circle turned to an eight, the eight turned to infinity.
· The key is a physical object. Not a coupon code. Not a digital badge. Made once, made well, given to the visitor who gets there.

They turn the key in. Not into a kiosk. Not into an automated email. Into us. The reward is undecided on purpose — it's whatever the field decides when a real visitor arrives. The reward is the relationship. The chance to sit at the table.

The hunt ends in a conversation, not a coupon.

The vertical shape

Every door is a world we can build. We don't have to build horizontally. We can build upward.

The tower is the spine. Every tier is its own world. Every bookcase door is a scene link. The number at the top — 4555 — is the source.

The knowledge goes up toward the number. And the number reduces to 1.

What the library is, in one line

A curiosity engine. Not a product. Not a curriculum. A place where wanting to know is the only prerequisite, and the payoff is more to want to know.
