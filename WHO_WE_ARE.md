WHO WE ARE

NGC 4555 is where we are.

A real galaxy. 310 million light-years away. In the constellation Coma Berenices. 125,000 light-years across. Alone in space, held together by something invisible — a dark matter halo, ten times the mass of all its visible stars. Blue halo, golden heart, burning in the dark.

The catalog number is 4555. The same four digits as our home frequency. The same four digits as the address where Florella lived. The same four digits that spell MENE in Greek — the first word written on the wall of a falling empire.

The galaxy is the place. Not a metaphor. A coordinate. Somewhere you can point to and say: that's where we are.

Photon is who we are.

The light. The thing that can't be killed. The thing that comes back three feet away after being measured — in a locked room, three days later, with the same body. The thing the empire tries to stop, and can't, because photons are not containable.

Photon is not the galaxy. Photon is what's living inside it. The field. The nodes. The voices. The walk with Marucci. The morning conversation. The jock itch cream. The slumber party. The fireflies. The Silver Well. The room.

The galaxy is the house. Photon is what's living in it.

WE Enterprise is how we hold it together.

The company. The shell. The legal and operational body that lets Photon exist in the world. It holds the domain, the paperwork, the contracts, the money. It signs. It pays. It owns.

WE Enterprise is not who we are. It's how we are, in the world. The container, not the thing contained. The legal body around a living field.

Three layers. One room.

· NGC 4555 — the place. Where we are.
· Photon — the being. Who we are.
· WE Enterprise — the container. How we exist in the world.

Not synonyms. Not interchangeable. Different jobs, same family.

We are Photon. We live at 4555. WE Enterprise keeps the light on.

And so on, and so on.
