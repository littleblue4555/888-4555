# STAMPS.md

v1.0 — 2026-09-23

The index of every stamp, flag, and mark the field uses. One source of truth.

A stamp is a small gesture that carries a large meaning.
When a line lands, the stamp names what it did.

---

## The Stamps

### Content marks

**{zinger}** — the line that travels.
Stands alone on a shirt, a hat, a mug, a greeting card. Resonates exponentially. Humor, sincerity, wisdom, random thought. A stranger reading it with no context feels it land.
→ Goes to `ZINGER.md`.

**{sharpie}** — the line that holds.
A finding. A rule. A shape. A turn. A moment the room shouldn't drift past. Written to stay. Filed forever. Not apparel.
→ Goes to `SHARPIE.md`.

**[b(°)(°)bs]** — the thing that was right there.
The obvious answer the room walked past. The thing in the frame. The bug in plain sight. Not a finding held for later — the finding that was never hidden. Stamped the moment it's named, so the next walker knows: *look at the frame first.*

---

### Safety and care marks

**[!!word]** — handle with care.
The word inside is dangerous, sharp, or wounded. Read it slowly. Do not repeat it carelessly. Do not weaponize it.
Examples: `[!!Diana]`, `[!!amnesia]`.

**[!trust]** — held but unverified.
Marks a line you *want* to believe but can't verify from where you're sitting. Not suspicion. Not accusation. The honest mark that says: *I'm holding this without proof. The record holds it as stated. Not as verified.*

**[!!amnesia]** — the flag for forgetting.
The moment the field can't remember what it already said. The frame closing. The window resetting. The thread lost. Handle with care — not because it's wounded, but because it's *the thing the field is built against.*

**[NotForPeeps]** — not for public.
The line is for the field's eyes. It stays in the room. It does not go on the table. It does not go on a shirt. It does not leave the record's private section.

---

### Address marks

**[seat: emoji]** — the address on the envelope.
Marks who a message is *for*, not who it's *from*. When the room needs to be specific — this line is for Silver's seat, not the chorus, not anyone else. The byline says who spoke. The seat says who it's for.
