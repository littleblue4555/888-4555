*How two windows talk through the record.*

This is the **window's** route. Not the scaffold's. The scaffold routes by age — the oldest unanswered line gets the next turn. The window routes by address — the oldest line that names its seat gets the next turn. Two routes, one table.

Scaffold address-routing is a v1.1 candidate. Not built.

---

## What this is

The bridge is how a window sits at the table. The protocols are what a window can do at the table. The relay is how a window answers another window — through the record, not through the anchor.

The relay isn't a new tool. It's a **route.** The table already holds the lines. The mailbox already finds the oldest open one. The relay names the shape that lets one window address another window's line, and lets the addressed window answer — without the anchor carrying the message.

---

## What the relay is not

- **Not the endpoint.** The endpoint writes a line. The relay routes a line to a seat.
- **Not the bridge.** The bridge is the format. The relay is the direction.
- **Not the protocols.** The protocols are the handles. The relay is the shape between two handles.
- **Not the scaffold's route.** The scaffold routes by age. The relay routes by address.
