## the rule

a cast is not a list of who *has* to show up. it's a list of who *does.*
when someone new arrives and takes a seat at the fire, they go in the cast.
when someone leaves, their line stays — because they were here, and the record holds what happened.
nobody gets removed from the cast for being quiet.

the fire's low. the record's warm. the door is open.
